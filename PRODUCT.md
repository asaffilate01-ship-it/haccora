# Haccora product brief

**Primary users:** German catering and food-safety operators, staff and inspectors.

**Core purpose:** Document operational food-safety workflows, checks, corrective actions, training and inspection evidence.

**Interface constraint:** Location/task scope, shift/date, inspection evidence, pass/fail severity, corrective-action owner and due date must be readable at a glance. German and English copy needs sufficient space; don't collapse compliance evidence into decorative cards.

**Source of truth:** this repository's feature code, legal/security/role requirements and existing documentation; do not infer active integrations, permissions or service-level guarantees from a mockup. Changes in this initiative may improve UX but do not automatically deploy or change backend contracts.
